SAMPLE: Countries are becoming more and more similar because people are able to buy
the same products anywhere in the world. Do you think this is a positive or negative
development?


It is said that countries are becoming similar to each other because of the global spread of the same
products, which are now available for purchase almost anywhere. I strongly believe that this modern
development is largely detrimental to culture and traditions worldwide.

A country’s history, language and ethos are all inextricably bound up in its manufactured artefact. If the
relentless advance of international brands into every corner of the world continues, these bland packages
might one day completely oust the traditional objects of a nation, which would be a loss of richness and
diversity in the world, as well as the sad disappearance of the manifestations of a place’s character. What
would a Japanese tea ceremony be without its specially crafted teapot, or a Fijian kava ritual without its
bowl made from a certain type of tree bark?

Let us not forget either that traditional products, whether these be medicines, cosmetics, toys, clothes,
utensils or food, provide employment for local people. The spread of multinational products can often
bring in its wake a loss of jobs, as people turn to buying the new brand, perhaps thinking it more
glamorous than the one they are used to. This eventually puts old-school craftspeople out of work. Finally,
tourism numbers may also be affected, as travelers become disillusioned with finding every place just the
same as the one they visited previously. To see the same products in shops the world over is boring, and
does not impel visitors to open their wallets in the same way that trinkets or souvenirs unique to the
particular area do.

Some may argue that all people are entitled to have access to the same products, but I say that local
objects suit local conditions best, and that faceless uniformity worldwide is an unwelcome and dreary
prospect.
SAMPLE: Some university students want to learn about other subjects in addition to their
main subjects. Others believe it is more important to give all their time and attention to
studying for a qualification. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.


Students attend university to improve their prospects and find suitable employment after
graduation. For this reason, some feel that they should focus all their energy on their main
subjects to gain a relevant qualification. Others want a more well-rounded education, so
they try to learn about additional subjects.

It is perfectly reasonable for students to enter university with a strong sense of curiosity and
a desire to learn as much as possible. Unfortunately, we tend to put subjects into artificial
boxes, suggesting that business, art and science are not connected. If students become too
focused on a single area, it may stifle their initial curiosity, limiting their potential. They
could also graduate with a very narrow skill set that doesn’t translate well to the current job
market, which often favors those who have taken a multidisciplinary approach to their
studies.

Despite this, caution is certainly needed. The more we learn about a subject, the more
complex it becomes. Gaining an in-depth, specialist knowledge of a subject requires a
certain level of focus and dedication over a long period. If we try to learn about too many
things at once, out knowledge may lack the depth required to obtain a qualification. If they
are not careful, young people could begin to lose interest in their main subjects, which
would be detrimental to their studies.

While learning about other subjects is not necessarily a bad thing, I believe university
students should ensure that their main subjects remain the priority so that they do not lose
sight of their objective: gaining a qualification. Then they can calculate how much time,
energy and head-space they have left for learning about other topics.
SAMLE: In many countries, a high proportion of criminal acts are committed by
teenagers. What are the reasons and what do you suggest to solve this problem？

It’s said that a great number of criminal acts are made by teenagers on a global scale. From
my perspective, lack of domestic education and the misguidance from mainstream media
are two contributory factors triggering the phenomenon. However, such issue can be
alleviated through several approaches.

Firstly poor regulation in schools results in a fact that children have difficulties in
distinguishing with the right and wrong at their early stage, which in the wake of the
absence in the awareness of obeying the law. In this situation, teenager might carry out
errant behaviour unconsciously, which may lead them to the way of committing crimes. In
addition, based on the mismanagement from school, misguided information produced by
social media may further accelerate the situation. Being surrounded by the unbefitting
messages from the press not only creates an improper environment for teenagers, this
publicity, even worse, may make them have a desire to imitate such inappropriate behaviour,
such as being violent, which in fact are incorrect. Under such circumstance, the mislead of
the Internet information might accelerate the possibility of juvenile delinquency.

Reinforcing the school management and strengthening supervision from media, to a large
extent, can mitigate and even prohibit the occurrence of the problem. Firstly institutes
adapting series of policies, such as organizing lectures, printing brochures, can help
establish awareness of complying with the law. Once they can tell from the right and wrong,
being subject to the sense of humiliation, teenagers would not continue to make mistakes,
thus reduce the amount of illegal performance. Beyond the above, mass media
strengthening their control of incorrect information can lessen the opportunity of being
exposed by the inappropriate information. Based on this, young people will be less affected
by these negative contents, so that the phenomenon of high proportion of juvenile
delinquency can be addressed.

The issue of law-breaking generated by adolescent does not caused by a single reason, and
the main contributory factors are the lack of education in schools as well as the mislead of
publicity. However, these problems can be overcome through the above actions.
SAMPLE: It is important for children to learn the difference between right and wrong at an
early age.Punishment is necessary to help them learn this distinction. To what extent do you
agree or disagree with this opinion? What sort of punishment should parents and teachers be
allowed to use to teach good behaviour to children?

One important stage in a child’s growth is certainly the development of a conscience, which
is linked to the ability to tell right from wrong. This skill comes with time and good
parenting, and my firm conviction is that punishment does not have much of a role to play
in this. Therefore, I have to disagree almost entirely with the given statement.

To some extent the question depends on the age of the child. To punish a very young child is
both wrong and foolish, as an infant will not understand what is happening or why he or she
is being punished. Once the age of reason is reached however, a child can be rewarded for
good behaviour and discouraged from bad. This kind but firm approach will achieve more
than harsh punishments, which might entail many negative consequences unintended by the
parents.

To help a child learn the difference between right and wrong, teachers and parents should
firstly provide good role modelling in their own behaviour. After that, if sanctions are
needed, the punishment should not be of a physical nature, as that merely sends the message
that it is acceptable for larger people to hit smaller ones - an outcome which may well result
in the child starting to bully others. Nor should the punishment be in any way cruel.

Rather, teachers and parents can use a variety of methods to discipline their young charges,
such as detention, withdrawal of privileges, and time-out. Making the punishment fit the
crime is a useful notion, which would see children being made to pick up rubbish they have
dropped, clean up graffiti they have drawn, or apologize to someone they have hurt. In these
ways responsibility is developed in the child, which leads to much better future behaviour
than does punishment.

- PARAGRAPH 1 - Introduction (观点类&讨论类大作文)
主要内容：改写标题交代背景前提
     表达核心立场/中心观点

具体要求：改写话题不能照抄题目，需要体现一定的变化
     核心立场的表达需要足够明确
     整体篇幅不宜过长，一般 2-3 句话

话题改写方式：
  方法 1：用 whether 引导宾语从句/主语从句+近义词替换
  题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
       service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
  改写： Some people have recently wondered whether all students in high schools
       would be benefit from joining in volunteer work.
  分析： Whether 引导宾语从句
       Should be encouraged → would be benefit from
       Community service programme → volunteer work

    方法 2：用 it is ...... that 结构引导主语从句+近义词替换
    题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
         service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
    改写： It is said that all students in high schools would be benefit from joining in
         volunteer work.
    分析： It is...... that......引导主语从句
         Should be encouraged → would be benefit from
         Community service programme → volunteer work

    方法 3：固定句式：People currently hold different views revolving if + TOPIC
    题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
         service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
    改写： People currently hold different views revolving if all students in high schools
         would be benefit from joining in volunteer work.
    分析： People currently hold different views revolving if
         Should be encouraged → would be benefit from
         Community service programme → volunteer work
核心观点表达方式：
  方法 1：From my perspective, ...... takes much of the credit on......
  题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
       service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
  立场： Students participating in unpaid work takes much of the credit on themselves.

    方法 2：My firm conviction is that......
    题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
         service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
    立场： My firm conviction is that students participating in unpaid work takes much
         of the credit on themselves.

    方法 3：eschew A in favour of B
    题目： All high school students should be encouraged to take part in community
         service programme. To what extent do you agree or disagree this statement?
    立场： As for my opinion, I eschew the opposite in favour of participating in the
         unpaid work

2. The world of work is changing rapidly. Working conditions today are not the same as
   before and people no longer rely on taking one job for life. Discuss the possible causes of
   these changes and give your suggestions on how people should prepare for work in the
   future.
     Causes:
     Educational System Focus on Individual Achievement:
     Many educational institutions emphasize individual performance over collaborative work. Students are often assessed
     based on their individual assignments, exams, and projects, which fosters a competitive environment rather than a coop
     erative one. This focus on personal achievement means that students have fewer opportunities to develop teamwork an
     d interpersonal skills.

      Digital Communication Over Face-to-Face Interaction:
      With the rise of digital communication tools, students increasingly rely on texting, emails, and social media to interact.
      This reduces their opportunities to engage in face-to-face conversations and practice essential interpersonal skills. The
     lack of real-world interaction can hinder their ability to work effectively in a team setting once they enter the workforc
e.

     Solutions:
     Integrate Team Projects in the Curriculum:
     Educational institutions should incorporate more team-based projects and collaborative assignments into their curriculu
     m. By working in groups, students can develop and enhance their interpersonal skills, learn to navigate group dynamic
     s, and practice effective communication. This practical experience will prepare them for the collaborative nature of mo
     st workplaces.

     Workshops and Training Programs:
     Schools and universities should offer workshops and training programs focused on interpersonal skills, such as active li
     stening, conflict resolution, and teamwork. These programs can provide students with the tools and techniques needed t
     o work well with others. Additionally, institutions can partner with businesses to offer internships and real-world exper
     iences where students can apply these skills in a professional setting.


     Causes:
     Technological Advancements:
     Rapid advancements in technology have transformed the workplace. Automation, artificial intelligence, and digital
     tools have changed the nature of many jobs, making some obsolete while creating new ones. This constant evolution
     requires workers to adapt and acquire new skills continuously.

     Globalization and Economic Shifts:
     Globalization has led to more interconnected economies, where businesses operate across borders. This has increased
     competition and driven companies to adapt quickly to changing market conditions. As a result, the traditional notion of
      a lifelong job with one company has become less prevalent, with more people experiencing multiple career changes
     throughout their lives.

     Solutions:
     Lifelong Learning and Skill Development:
     Individuals should embrace lifelong learning to stay relevant in the workforce. This includes continuously updating
     their skills through formal education, online courses, and professional development programs. Staying informed about
     industry trends and acquiring new competencies can help workers adapt to changing job requirements and secure new
     opportunities.

     Flexibility and Adaptability:
     Preparing for the future of work requires a mindset shift towards flexibility and adaptability. Workers should be open
     to exploring different career paths and be willing to pivot when necessary. Building a diverse skill set and gaining
     experience in various roles can make individuals more resilient to changes in the job market. Networking and
     maintaining professional relationships can also provide support and opportunities in a rapidly evolving work
     environment.
