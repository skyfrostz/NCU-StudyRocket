Detai-2
In the planned development, the countryside will be replaced
by a dual carriageway encircling the town center.

The shops currently along the main road will be expanded into
a series of new facilities, including a bus station, shopping
center, and car park. The new layout also designates the area
adjacent to these shops as a pedestrian-only zone.

The main road that currently runs through the center of the
town will be transformed to accommodate pedestrians
exclusively, enhancing the town's walkability. Additionally, new
housing areas will be developed both north and south of this
pedestrian zone, replacing some of the existing housing.

Other notable changes include the introduction of a park area
to the southeast, adjacent to the new housing, and the
continued presence of the school in the southwest, which will
remain in its current location but be connected to the dual
carriageway for better accessibility.
